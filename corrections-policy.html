Corrections Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

We aim to get things right. When we do not, we correct the record promptly and transparently.

How to report an error

Email corrections@example.com with the article URL, the passage you believe is wrong and, where possible, a source supporting the correction. We aim to respond within two working days.

How we correct

  • Factual errors are corrected in the article, and a note at the end of the article explains what was changed and when.
  • Clarifications are added where the original wording was accurate but could be misread.
  • Minor typographical errors that do not change meaning are fixed without a note.
  • The 'last updated' date on the article reflects the correction.

Headlines and social media

If a correction affects a headline or a social media post, we update the headline and publish a follow-up post where appropriate.

Removal of articles

We do not remove published articles except in exceptional circumstances, such as a legal requirement or a serious risk to safety. Article URLs are permanent.
